Aurora — logo explorations

The Latin aurora: the moments before dawn. A circle split at the horizon: half sky, half sea. In the sky the first light rises from ember red to night blue; the sea holds its reflection. A small four-point star (the morning star) appears in some versions.

Gradient dawn

Smooth sky from night blue to ember, light pooling under the horizon, its glow reflected on the sea.

Malibu bars

Sky and sea as solid colour bands, tightening towards the horizon like compressed light.

Malibu flat

Same bands without gaps: a quieter, poster-like reading that holds up better at small sizes.

Hybrid

Gradient sky where the aurora lives, Malibu bands for the sea, with the dawn's reflection running down them.
